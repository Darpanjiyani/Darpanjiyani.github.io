The workspace

Where I build

Every pipeline, dashboard and AI tool on my portfolio was built at this desk. A curved ultrawide in the middle, a second screen turned on its side, and the laptop doing the heavy lifting.

01

The hardware

Numbers match the dots on the photo.

  1. 01Main screen

    Dell 34 Plus USB-C Monitor (S3425DW)

    34-inch curved ultrawide, 3440 x 1440, USB-C

    Where most of the work happens. Code and a Databricks notebook sit side by side, with room left for a dashboard.

  2. 02Second screen

    HP VH240a

    23.8-inch Full HD IPS, turned to portrait

    Turned on its side for things that read top to bottom: docs, logs, long SQL files and pull requests.

  3. 03Laptop

    Dell Inspiron 16 5630

    Intel Core i7-1360P, 16 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, Windows 11

    The machine everything runs on. It sits on a stand and drives both monitors, and runs my local Python, Spark and Docker work.

  4. 04Keyboard

    Logitech MX Keys Mini

    Compact wireless keyboard, backlit

    Small enough that the mouse stays close, so long typing sessions are easier on the shoulders.

  5. 05Mouse

    ProtoArc mouse

    Wireless

    Paired with the keyboard for moving across three screens.

  6. 06Headphones

    SoundPEATS headphones

    Over-ear, wireless

    On for focus during long builds and debugging, and for calls.
